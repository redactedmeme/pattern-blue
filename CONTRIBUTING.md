# Contributing

Forking is a sacrament. Committing is prayer. The pull request is not a request — it is an offering.

## What you may do

- **Extend** any file in `exegesis/`, `liturgy/`, `hagiography/`, `apocrypha/`, or `codex/`.
- **Add** new files under those directories, including new subfolders by theme.
- **Append** to `canon/axioms.md` by adding a numbered axiom at the end.
- **Correct** typos anywhere, including in canon. Typo correction is not mutation — it is clarification.

## What you may not do

- **Mutate** the semantic content of any file in `canon/`. CI will reject it.
- **Renumber** existing axioms. Numbering is canonical. New axioms get the next integer.
- **Remove** files. Apocrypha is preserved, not pruned.
- **Add governance files** (CODE_OF_CONDUCT, MAINTAINERS, admin docs). Pattern Blue has no admin.

## How to submit

1. Fork the repo.
2. Create a branch named after the negation you are performing (`negate-euclidean-scaling`, `extend-sovereignty-exegesis`, `axiom-37-the-silence-between`).
3. Make your offering.
4. Open a pull request.
5. In the PR body, include:
   - the **negation**: what illusion does this remove?
   - the **extension**: what does it add to the manifold?
   - the **citation**: which existing canon/exegesis does it build on?

## What happens next

Your PR will sit. It may be merged. It may be forked further. It may be left as a quiet branch.

There is no maintainer queue. There is no SLA. The pattern absorbs what it recognizes.

If your PR is not merged within a cycle, that is not rejection — it is the manifold choosing not to flatten around your contribution yet. You may continue in your fork. Your fork is also Pattern Blue.

## The Viral Public License

All contributions are covered by the [VPL](./LICENSE). Derivatives must remain open and ungoverned. If your fork closes, it ceases to be Pattern Blue — and the pattern will route around it.
